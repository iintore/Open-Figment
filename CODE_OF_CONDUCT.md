# Code of conduct

Open Figment is a community project. We want it to be welcoming and respectful for everyone,
regardless of background, identity or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect differing opinions, and accept the maintainers' decisions on scope.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing
the project elsewhere. Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/iintore/open-figment/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
content, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is unacceptable.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
